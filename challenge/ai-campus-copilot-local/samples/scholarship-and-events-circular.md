## Part A — Scholarships

### A.1 Institute Merit Scholarship

- **Value:** Full tuition waiver for one academic year
- **Eligibility:** Students entering the third or fourth year with a CGPA of 8.5 or
  above and no history of disciplinary action.
- **Required documents:** Consolidated grade sheet, income certificate, Aadhaar copy
- **Application deadline:** 30 April 2027
- **Announcement of results:** Expected in the second week of June 2027

Applications are submitted through the Student Welfare portal. Incomplete applications
will be rejected without further correspondence.

### A.2 Rural Access Grant

- **Value:** ₹40,000 per academic year
- **Eligibility:** Students whose permanent residence is in a village or town with a
  population below 20,000, and whose annual family income does not exceed ₹3,00,000.
- **Required documents:** Residence certificate issued by the local revenue authority,
  income certificate issued within the last twelve months
- **Application deadline:** 22 May 2027
- **Interview for shortlisted candidates:** 8 June 2027

Shortlisted candidates will be informed by email at least seven days before the
interview date.

### A.3 Sports Excellence Award

- **Value:** ₹25,000 one-time award and a hostel fee concession of 50%
- **Eligibility:** Students who have represented the state or the institute at a
  national-level tournament during the 2026–27 academic year.
- **Required documents:** Participation or merit certificate attested by the Director
  of Physical Education
- **Application deadline:** 15 May 2027

A student may hold only one scholarship from Part A at a time. Students receiving an
external government scholarship must declare it at the time of application.

## Part B — Campus events

### B.1 Aarambh 2027 — Annual Technical Symposium

- **Dates:** 24 and 25 April 2027
- **Venue:** Central Seminar Hall and departmental laboratories
- **Registration deadline:** 18 April 2027
- **Registration fee:** ₹300 per participant, ₹800 per team of four

Events include a paper presentation contest, a 24-hour hackathon, a robotics challenge
and a technical quiz. Certificates will be issued to all registered participants who
attend both days.

### B.2 Open Library Week

- **Dates:** 3 to 7 June 2027
- **Venue:** Central Library

Extended library hours from 08:00 to 22:00. Borrowing limits are raised from three to
six books for the duration of the week. All borrowed books must be returned by
**14 June 2027** to avoid a late fee of ₹5 per book per day.

### B.3 Blood Donation Camp

- **Date:** 12 June 2027
- **Venue:** Health Centre, Ground Floor
- **Timing:** 09:00 to 15:00

Registration is on the day of the camp. Donors must be between 18 and 60 years of age
and weigh at least 50 kg.

## Part C — Fee reminders

The first instalment of the 2027–28 tuition fee is payable on or before
**15 July 2027**. A late payment charge of ₹100 per day applies from 16 July 2027.
Hostel residents must additionally clear mess dues before **30 June 2027** to retain
