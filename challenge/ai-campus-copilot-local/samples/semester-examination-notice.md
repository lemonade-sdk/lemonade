## 1. Examination period

The end-semester theory examinations for the even semester will be conducted from
**Monday, 10 May 2027 to Saturday, 29 May 2027**. Examinations will be held in two
sessions each day:

| Session | Reporting time | Examination time |
|---------|----------------|------------------|
| Forenoon | 09:15 | 09:30 – 12:30 |
| Afternoon | 13:45 | 14:00 – 17:00 |

Practical examinations will be held from **31 May 2027 to 5 June 2027**. The
department-wise practical timetable will be published separately by each department.

## 2. Examination fee

All candidates must pay the examination fee through the student portal.

- Regular fee: **₹1,200** per student, payable on or before **20 March 2027**.
- Late fee window: **21 March 2027 to 28 March 2027**, with an additional **₹500** penalty.
- No application will be accepted after 28 March 2027 under any circumstances.

Candidates who have pending semester tuition dues will not be permitted to pay the
examination fee until those dues are cleared.

## 3. Hall tickets

Hall tickets will be released on the student portal on **1 May 2027**. Candidates must
carry a printed hall ticket and their institute identity card to every examination.
Entry will not be permitted without both documents.

## 4. Attendance eligibility

A candidate is eligible to appear for the end-semester examination in a course only if
the candidate has attended at least **75%** of the scheduled classes in that course.

Candidates with attendance between **65% and 74%** may apply for condonation by
submitting the prescribed form to the Head of Department along with supporting
documents on or before **15 April 2027**. Condonation is granted at the discretion of
the Academic Committee and is not automatic.

Candidates with attendance below 65% are not eligible and must repeat the course.

## 5. Revaluation

Results are expected to be published on **28 June 2027**. Applications for revaluation
must be submitted within **fifteen days** of the result publication date, along with a
fee of ₹400 per course.

## 6. Examination conduct

- Mobile phones, smart watches and programmable calculators are prohibited in the
  examination hall.
- Candidates arriving more than 30 minutes after the start of a session will not be
  admitted.
- Any candidate found using unfair means will be dealt with under the Institute
  Examination Malpractice Rules, 2024.
